## Scientific takeaway

### 中文导读

Zhang 系统综述 FRB 的现象学、基本等离子体物理、辐射机制、源与环境模型、传播效应和宇宙学探针，并明确保留多个未解决的起源问题。 该模型中的一个代表性关系写作 $$\Delta t\propto {\rm DM}\,\nu^{-2}$$，它用于说明变量之间的物理联系，并不脱离本文的模型设定独立成立。

### English reading note

The abstract and model report the result within that scope. A representative relation is $$\Delta t\propto {\rm DM}\,\nu^{-2}$$; it is retained as a model equation, not promoted to an unqualified universal law.

- The data selection and parameter range limit how far the reported result can be generalized.
