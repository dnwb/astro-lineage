# Distinguish explicit and inferred Edges

Every Edge records whether its relationship is explicitly stated by a concrete Version or inferred by a curator. An Explicit Edge may be self-reviewed by its creator against the cited text; an Inferred Edge requires Bilateral Evidence and independent review by a second curator. A Material Change to the relation, reason, or Evidence resets the Review State to unreviewed, preserving useful synthesis without presenting interpretation as an author-stated fact.
