## 中文导读

这个方向追踪中心天体储存的能量如何进入膨胀抛射物，再经由激波、绝热冷却和辐射扩散形成快速而明亮的瞬变。阅读顺序从经典解析光变曲线开始，经过并合新星和磁星模型，最后进入把发动机、激波与扩散耦合起来的当前方法。

## English reading note

This line follows energy stored in a compact central object into expanding ejecta, then through shocks, adiabatic cooling, and radiative diffusion into fast luminous transients. It moves from a classic analytic light-curve solution through merger-nova and magnetar models to current engine–shock–diffusion methods.
