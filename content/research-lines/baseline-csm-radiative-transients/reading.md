## 中文导读

这个方向从自相似激波和星风泡的基础解开始，进入星周介质—抛射物相互作用的形式化描述，再连接到时间依赖辐射扩散、射电诊断和群体比较。它特别适合区分激波产生的能量、光子输运过程以及从光变拟合得到的推断量。

## English reading note

This line begins with self-similar shocks and wind-blown bubbles, then develops a formal CSM–ejecta interaction picture before connecting it to time-dependent diffusion, radio diagnostics, and population comparisons. It separates shock-generated energy, photon transport, and quantities inferred from light-curve fitting.
