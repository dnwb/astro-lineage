# The Physics of Fast Radio Bursts

## Why This Work Matters

The definitive, comprehensive treatise on fast radio burst physics. It provides an exhaustive map of coherent plasma radiation mechanisms, source environments, propagation diagnostics, and cosmological applications.

## Problem

What plasma physics mechanism drives coherent emission with brightness temperatures exceeding $10^{35}\,{\rm K}$, and how can intervening propagation effects be disentangled from source environment physics?

## Scientific takeaway

Compares magnetospheric vs far-away shock emission models. Establishes the precise dispersion delay relation $$\Delta t \propto {\rm DM}\,\nu^{-2}$$, Faraday rotation RM diagnostics, plasma scattering limits, and cosmological baryon census methodologies.

## Assumptions

- Requires coherent emission via charge bunching or relativistic plasma maser instabilities.
- Propagation modeled via cold plasma dispersion relations through ionized interstellar and intergalactic media.
- Allows for possible multiple progenitor channels distinguishing repeaters and non-repeaters.

## Scientific delta

Transmutes fragmented empirical discoveries into an integrated five-tier causal architecture: engine, acceleration, coherent radiation, propagation, and cosmological probes.

## Reason to read

Standard authoritative handbook for FRB researchers. Focus on the mathematical decomposition of dispersion measure (DM) and coherent bunching constraints.
