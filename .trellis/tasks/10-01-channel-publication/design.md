Reader extension: presentation-only Chinese labels map the existing authoritative direction IDs; no canonical meanings change. A Chinese research-question headline is an editorial reading title, not a fabricated translation of the paper title; retain the original title in provenance. Daily briefing preserves validated scientific prose and Must Read sentences verbatim; only the trailing navigation phrase `；其余见下方卡片。` may be replaced with `。`, matching the website's existing `cleanBriefSummary`. Weekly uses its existing owned identity and validated export text. Each publication component reports its own result; a missing chart/brief cannot authorize weaker paper evidence. Chart/brief recovery after ledger loss requires a complete remote inventory and exactly one self-consistent same-identity marker, regardless of its prior body hash; duplicates remain pending and external edits are never overwritten.

Keep paper publication bound to the immutable current generation. A bad archive leaves only that brief pending. This prevents archive editorial updates from being overwritten by an older current-generation opening brief on each normal reentry.

Remote layout verification checks the group-reference surface, while full-body hash verification still checks all scientific text.
