## 中文导读

这条路径先建立相对论爆炸波和余辉的动力学基础，再学习喷流头部与茧状体，随后把传播问题放入活动星系核吸积盘，最后进入受阻喷流和动态中微子产额。

## English reading note

This path builds relativistic blast-wave and afterglow foundations, introduces jet-head and cocoon dynamics, places propagation in an AGN disk, and ends with choked jets and dynamic neutrino yields.
