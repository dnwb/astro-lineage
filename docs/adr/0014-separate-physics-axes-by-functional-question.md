# Separate physics axes by functional question

The Physics Ontology separates central object, energy reservoir, and energy transfer; separates energy dissipation, particle interaction, emission, and transport; and separates phenomenon, messenger, photon band, and observable. An absent central object is allowed, observables contain only actual measurement forms rather than inferred parameters, and microscopic processes may occupy different Functional Roles when justified instead of being forced into philosophically absolute partitions.
