# Model Causal Links separately from Scientific Edges

Physical Account arrows are first-class Work-local Causal Links that may carry reason, Evidence, origin, Interpretive Risk, and Review State. They reuse the explicit or inferred origin vocabulary while retaining separate Evidence and review rules. Their relation vocabulary and governance remain separate from Work-to-Work Scientific Edges so a paper-lineage assertion cannot be confused with a causal relationship inside one Work's physical account.
