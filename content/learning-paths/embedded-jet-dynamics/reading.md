# From Jet Propagation to Dynamic Multi-messenger Yields

This path moves from a compact account of relativistic jet propagation to an
embedded AGN-disk setting, and then to multi-messenger applications with
characteristic-state and trajectory-resolved extensions.

Begin with Bromberg et al. 2011 to establish jet-head motion, cocoon
collimation, and breakout as the basic propagation vocabulary. Read Zhang et al.
2024 next to apply that vocabulary to the dense and finite geometry of an AGN
accretion disk. Follow with Zhu et al. 2021 to examine how disk confinement
redirects jet energy into multi-messenger channels, with choked jets, hadronic
interaction branches, and a neutrino messenger. Finish with Long and Yu 2026 to
examine how evolving shock and cooling conditions are followed along complete
jet-head trajectories through choking or breakout.

The three transitions are path-local pedagogical reasons for this order. They do
not assert scientific lineage, add a Paper Graph Edge, or create a global
next-read relation between Works.
