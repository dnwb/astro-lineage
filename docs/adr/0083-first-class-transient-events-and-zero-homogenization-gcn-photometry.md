# First-Class Transient Events and Zero-Homogenization GCN Photometry

Astrophysical transients such as `GRB 261029A / AT 2026ahaw` receive rapid, unrefereed follow-up reports via NASA GCN Circulars days before any arXiv `Work` is published, and those reports mix heterogeneous photometric systems (`AB`, `Vega`, unfiltered `Clear`/`L`), X-ray fluxes, radio flux densities, and evolving redshift constraints without uniform Galactic extinction corrections. We therefore model `Transient Event` as a first-class domain entity independent of `Work` (with cross-facility alias unification and a deterministic canonical trigger epoch $T_0$), and we preserve and plot GCN photometry and multi-messenger measurements in their native reported physical units across shared-$\Delta t$ panels rather than forcing synthetic cross-band or cross-messenger flux conversions. Missing uncertainties, approximate epochs, and cross-circular version conflicts are surfaced explicitly via open markers and callout annotations (`Observational Discrepancy`) rather than silently filtered or averaged.
