# Why this Work matters

Zhu et al. connect a dense AGN accretion disk to a multi-messenger transient
account. A GRB jet stalls inside the disk, its reverse shock accelerates
protons, and two hadronic interaction branches converge on meson decay and a
high-energy-neutrino signal.

## Scientific takeaway

The Physical Account keeps the carriers and measurements separate. Neutrinos,
photons, and a predicted short-GRB gravitational-wave counterpart are
messengers; soft gamma rays are a photon band; fluence and detector counts are
observable forms; jet choking and expected yield remain model-derived
inference targets.
