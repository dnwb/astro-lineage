# Separate editorial context from scientific ontology

Research Lines, their independently reviewed Work memberships, Reading Roles, and Editorial Anchors belong entirely to the Editorial Layer rather than to the scientific ontology. Each Work–Line pair has at most one membership with a non-empty deduplicated role set; every visible Work has exactly one Anchor membership to a visible Line, and every visible Line has a reviewed membership to a visible Work. Eligible Human creators may self-review memberships, while Agent-created memberships require Human review; changing a membership's Work, roles, or Anchor designation resets its review and invalidates affected visibility approval. These constraints organize publication without becoming intrinsic scientific properties of the Work.
