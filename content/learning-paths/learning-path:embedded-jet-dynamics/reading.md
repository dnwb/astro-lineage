# From Jet Propagation to Dynamic Multi-messenger Yields

This path moves from a compact account of relativistic jet propagation to an
embedded AGN-disk multi-messenger application, and then to a
trajectory-resolved extension of that application.

Begin with Bromberg et al. 2011 to establish jet-head motion, cocoon
collimation, and breakout as the basic propagation vocabulary. Read Zhu et al.
2021 next to follow that vocabulary into a dense AGN-disk setting with choked
jets, hadronic interaction branches, and a neutrino messenger. Finish with
Long and Yu 2026 to examine how evolving shock and cooling conditions are
followed along complete jet-head trajectories through choking or breakout.

The two transitions are path-local pedagogical reasons for this order. They do
not assert scientific lineage, add a Paper Graph Edge, or create a global
next-read relation between Works.
