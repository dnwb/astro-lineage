## 中文导读

这个方向把双星轨道几何、脉冲星风激波和磁化环境连接到射电、X 射线和高能瞬变。它从综述和具体双星建模开始，再进入 FRB 的环境约束、磁星情景和长期旋转量变化，适合学习如何把传播效应转化成物理诊断。

## English reading note

This line connects binary geometry, pulsar-wind shocks, and magnetized environments to radio, X-ray, and high-energy transients. It moves from a review and concrete binary models into FRB environmental constraints, magnetar scenarios, and long-term rotation-measure variations.
