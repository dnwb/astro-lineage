# Why this Work matters

Long and Yu follow an embedded GRB jet through an AGN accretion disk with a
time-dependent jet-head trajectory. The evolving head state controls the
reverse-shock dissipation, the collisionless acceleration window, and the
neutrino-producing interaction history until the jet chokes or breaks out.

## Scientific takeaway

The Work resolves the propagation history instead of replacing it with one
representative jet state. In choked cases, the stalling state captures the
dissipation-dominant phase to about 14 percent in the reported comparison;
breakout cases can be overpredicted by single-state estimates when the head
accelerates through steep density gradients. Lower-density outer-disk cases can
develop hard neutrino tails near 100 PeV.
