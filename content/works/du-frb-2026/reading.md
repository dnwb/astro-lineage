# Super-Orbital Variations in Magnetar Rotation Measure Arising from the Precession of Companion Star: Implications for FRB 20220529

## Why This Work Matters

A breakthrough paper establishing FRBs as precision astrophysical probes of magnetized binary environments (R5). It resolves the mysterious long-timescale rotation measure (RM) reversal of FRB 20220529 via companion magnetic axis precession and wind geometry.

## Problem

Why does FRB 20220529 exhibit dramatic super-orbital RM reversals across hundreds of days while its dispersion measure (DM) remains nearly flat, and what binary magnetic geometry enables this decoupling?

## Scientific takeaway

Constructs a binary model with companion magnetic axis precession solving the Faraday rotation integral $${\rm RM}=0.81\int n_e B_{\parallel}\,dl$$. Proves that an 182-day precession period and $19^\circ$ tilt match the super-orbital RM reversal, while constant DM decisively excludes dense equatorial disk penetration.

## Assumptions

- FRB emitter is embedded in a binary system dominated by a magnetized companion star wind.
- RM evolution is driven by geometric polarity reversals of $B_\parallel$ rather than massive density fluctuations.
- Companion spin axis maintains stable gravitational torque-induced precession over the multi-year baseline.

## Scientific delta

Decouples RM reversals from DM variations, establishing super-orbital companion precession as a rigorous physical mechanism for interpreting complex magneto-ionic environments of FRBs.

## Reason to read

Essential for understanding polarization diagnostics of FRB environments. Focus on the vector projection integration of $B_\parallel$ along the three-dimensional precessing line of sight.
