# Explosive Transients and CSM Interaction

This Research Line follows how explosive ejecta, internal heating, expansion,
radiative diffusion, and interaction with surrounding material shape transient
emission. A Work can contribute a method to this line without claiming that
every application contains circumstellar interaction.

## Why TransFit is anchored here

TransFit develops the time-dependent diffusion and model-data comparison layer
used to connect explosive-ejecta physics with observed bolometric light curves.
Its central-engine heating option also supports a secondary reading context in
the Central Engines line.
