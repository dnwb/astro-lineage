# Annual reading notebooks

Automatically maintain one annual NotebookLM notebook of published daily guides and weekly reports. Reuse existing annual notebooks; never alter research notebooks or canonical content. Preserve reading scope, evidence, pending status and formulas. Scheduled delivery must record website, channel, NotebookLM and QQ independently; QQ remains waiting for proactive permission.

Acceptance: offline build exports only built archive pages; calendar/ISO-year rollover and unchanged reentry are deterministic; a failed replacement preserves the old source; repeated delivery does not duplicate an unchanged source; credentials remain local and untracked. Live Google authentication and source readiness are separate environment-dependent acceptance gates.
