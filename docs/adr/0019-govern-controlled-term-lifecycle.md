# Govern the Controlled Term lifecycle

Controlled Terms, including Physics terms, Method Families, and Canonical Techniques, move from proposed to active to deprecated. Activation requires an independent Human reviewer, a definition, examples, counterexamples, boundary notes, and allowed Functional Roles where applicable. Canonical IDs are immutable, renaming uses labels or aliases, and deprecation records a reason while requiring a Successor Term only for genuine semantic replacement. Historical references are retained rather than hard-deleted.
