## 中文导读

这条路径从伽马射线双星综述和脉冲星风激波开始，进入 FRB 的等离子体物理和年轻磁星情景，最后用旋转量变化和掩食磁星爆发检验环境诊断的具体做法。

## English reading note

This path starts with gamma-ray binaries and pulsar-wind shocks, moves through FRB plasma physics and young-magnetar scenarios, and ends with concrete environmental diagnostics from rotation-measure changes and eclipsed magnetar bursts.
