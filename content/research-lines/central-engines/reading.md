# Central Engines and Engine-powered Transients

This Research Line follows the causal path from a physical energy reservoir,
through transfer and transport, to a transient signal that can be measured.

Arnett 1982 is the foundation entry here because it supplies a compact example:
radioactive decay deposits energy in expanding ejecta, radiative diffusion
transports that energy, and the model connects the account to optical light
curves. The Editorial Anchor organizes this reading context; it is not a claim
that the Work has one uniquely primary scientific classification.
