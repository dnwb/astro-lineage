# Use a two-level Method Taxonomy

Methods are organized as Method Family followed by Canonical Technique, and a Work may use multiple families. Families and Techniques use the full Controlled Term lifecycle and activation governance. Works reference active Techniques through Method Annotations using a dedicated `explicit | inferred` basis and Version-specific Evidence rather than Physics Interpretive Risk; Explicit assignments permit eligible Human self-review, while Inferred assignments require a reason and distinct Human reviewer. Material changes to technique, basis, reason, or Evidence reset review, and every visible Work requires at least one reviewed Method Annotation. Model Character descriptors such as semi-analytic remain distinct from concrete techniques such as MCMC or hydrodynamics, preventing modeling level, algorithm, and study practice from being treated as interchangeable tags.
