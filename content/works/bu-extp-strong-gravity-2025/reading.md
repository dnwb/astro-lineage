# Probing the strong gravity region of black holes with eXTP

## Why This Work Matters

The flagship science white paper led by Qingcui Bu for the future eXTP mission. It establishes the authoritative predictive framework for probing strong gravity around black hole event horizons via joint spectral-timing and X-ray polarimetry.

## Problem

How can strong-field general relativistic effects, frame dragging, black hole spin, and corona geometry be cleanly decoupled near the ISCO using combined timing, spectroscopy, and X-ray polarimetry?

## Scientific takeaway

Formulates the relativistic reflection and ISCO radius mapping $$ r_{\rm ISCO} = f(a_*) \frac{GM}{c^2} $$. Numerical simulations of Lense-Thirring precession and polarization angle modulations prove that eXTP can constrain dimensionless spin $a_*$ to better than 1% precision.

## Assumptions

- Spacetime is described by the Kerr metric under standard general relativity.
- Accretion disk conforms to a Novikov-Thorne thin disk transitioning to plunging flow inside the ISCO.
- X-ray corona is parameterized by lamp-post or slab geometries illuminating the inner accretion disk.

## Scientific delta

Integrates relativistic reflection, timing, and polarimetry into a single self-consistent diagnostic framework, setting the scientific standard for testing strong-field gravity and black hole spin over the next decade.
