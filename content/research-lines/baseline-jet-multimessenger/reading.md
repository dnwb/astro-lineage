## 中文导读

这个方向从相对论爆炸波和余辉的基本动力学出发，逐步进入喷流传播、环境诊断以及中微子和引力波信号。它把“外流如何传播”与“观测者实际测到什么”放在同一条阅读线上，同时保留每篇工作的模型假设和适用范围。

## English reading note

This line starts with relativistic blast-wave and afterglow dynamics, then moves through jet propagation, environmental inference, and neutrino or gravitational-wave signals. It keeps propagation physics connected to observables while retaining the assumptions and scope of each Work.
