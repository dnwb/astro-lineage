# Dense Environments and Multi-messenger Transients

This Research Line follows how a surrounding medium changes an outflow before
the signal reaches an observer. It keeps the physical chain explicit:
environmental confinement changes jet dynamics, shocks accelerate particles,
microscopic interactions produce messengers, and detector-facing observables
remain separate from model-derived inference targets.

Zhu et al. 2021 is the Editorial Anchor for this context because its choked
AGN-disk jet branches into neutrino and photon signals while retaining a
predicted gravitational-wave counterpart for the short-GRB channel. The
anchor organizes this page; it does not claim a primary scientific class.
