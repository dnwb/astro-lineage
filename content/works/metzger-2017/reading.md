# 毫秒磁星出生连接 FRB、超亮超新星和长伽马暴

## Why This Work Matters

### 中文导读

它是天体物理中横跨 R1（磁星）、R2（GRB）、R5（FRB）三大方向的核心桥梁文献。提出大质量恒星核心坍缩产生的新生毫秒强磁星，能够在不同演化时间尺度上统一解释长暴、超亮超新星以及重复快速射电暴。

### English reading note

A seminal cross-cutting bridge linking R1, R2, and R5. It proposes that newborn millisecond magnetars unified under core-collapse explosions can sequentially power long GRBs, SLSNe, and persistent repeating FRBs across different evolutionary epochs.

## Problem

### 中文导读

重复快速射电暴（如 FRB 121102）伴随的致密持续射电源、极高旋转量以及与恒星形成矮星系的关联，能否与驱动超亮超新星的新生磁星模型实现物理自洽？

### English reading note

Can repeating fast radio bursts, persistent radio sources, and extreme rotation measures be physically reconciled with the newborn magnetar progenitor model of superluminous supernovae?

## Scientific takeaway

### 中文导读

构建了毫秒磁星出生的全生命周期物理图景：秒级驱动 GRB 喷流，天-月级驱动 SLSN 辐射，数十年后磁星风星云电离抛射物稀释，FRB 射电辐射摆脱自由-自由吸收 $$ \tau_{\rm ff} \propto n_e^2 T^{-3/2} \nu^{-2} L $$ 从而向外穿透泄漏，同时星云同步辐射形成致密持续射电源。

### English reading note

Constructs a chronological lifecycle for newborn magnetars: jet launching at seconds, SLSN optical peaks at months, and radio burst emergence after decades once free-free optical depth $$ \tau_{\rm ff} \propto n_e^2 T^{-3/2} \nu^{-2} L $$ drops, leaving a persistent synchrotron nebula.

## Assumptions

### 中文导读

- 中心磁星拥有初始自转周期 $P_0 \sim 1\text{ ms}$ 与极强表面偶极场 $B \sim 10^{14}-10^{15}\text{ G}$。
- 膨胀抛射物质量约为数个太阳质量且贫氢（剥层包层），内部被磁星风注入形成磁化重子贫化气泡。
- FRB 产生机制位于磁星磁层内部或风终止激波处，并假设抛射物电离态随辐射衰减自洽演化。

### English reading note

- Newborn magnetar with $P_0 \sim 1\text{ ms}$ and surface dipole $B \sim 10^{14}-10^{15}\text{ G}$.
- Stripped-envelope core collapse ejecting several solar masses into a low-density cavity.
- FRBs originate from magnetospheric bursts or shock masers traversing an expanding ionized nebula.

## Scientific delta

### 中文导读

打破了各瞬变源孤立研究的学科壁垒，首次用统一的磁星中心引擎及演化中的外部包层介质，跨越 10 个数量级的时间尺度将高能爆发与射电相干辐射联系起来。

### English reading note

Unifies transient astrophysics across ten orders of magnitude in time, linking high-energy prompt gamma-rays, optical diffusion peaks, and coherent radio transients to one evolving engine.

## Reason to read

### 中文导读

掌握多信使、多尺度物理跨方向 Hub 的典范文献；重点精读自由-自由吸收光深演化如何决定 FRB 射电信号首次露出的时间窗口计算。

### English reading note

Essential reading for understanding cross-domain physical hubs. Focus on how the time evolution of free-free optical depth dictates the visibility window of repeating FRBs.
